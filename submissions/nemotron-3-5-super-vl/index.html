About Mara

Mara Lindqvist is a Reykjavík-based photographer drawn to basalt coasts, Atlantic weather, and the quiet hour between night and day. Her work treats light as a material — fractured, reflected, or dissolved — across landscape, portrait, street, and abstract frames. Every image on this site is generated in the browser; none are photographs.
