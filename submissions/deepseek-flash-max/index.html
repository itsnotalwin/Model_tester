About — field notes

I am interested in the moment light becomes geology.

Mara Lindqvist is a Reykjavík-born photographer working where weather becomes structure. For fourteen years she has carried a camera into fissures, harbours and the last bus home, making pictures that treat light as a mineral — something quarried, fractured and held.

Her prints are made in small editions and her field notes are never tidy. MAXIMAL LIGHT gathers twelve recent exposures: basalt, salt, neon, skin, ink, and the interference between them.

— Mara, Reykjavík
