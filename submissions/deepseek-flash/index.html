The north is
not a backdrop.

Mara Lindqvist is a Reykjavík-based photographer working between documentary and abstraction. Her practice follows weather, memory and the small negotiations that happen between people.

She photographs with available light, long walks and a stubborn belief that a landscape is also a portrait. Recent work moves between black-sand coastlines, late trams, bakeries before opening, and pools of ink in still water.

Available for editorial, portrait and place-based commissions
