About

Mara Lindqvist

Mara Lindqvist is a Reykjavík-based photographer working at the edge of landscape, memory, and night light. Her portraits feel like half-remembered dreams; her landscapes feel like places that have been waiting for years. She shoots in small batches, mostly film, always chasing the hour when weather and light stop negotiating.
