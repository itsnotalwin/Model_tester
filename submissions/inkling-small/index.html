Mara Lindqvist

Mara Lindqvist is a Reykjavík-based fine-art photographer whose work sits at the Intersection of atmosphere and abstraction. Born in Iceland's capital, she has spent over a decade studying how light behaves at high latitudes — the long goldens of summer, the sharp blues of winter, the mist that lingers between seasons.

Her practice embraces portraiture, street observation, landscape and abstract form. She photographs alone and with small groups, often returning to the same locations until the atmosphere reveals something new. Her images have been shown in Reykjavík, Copenhagen, Berlin and Tokyo.
