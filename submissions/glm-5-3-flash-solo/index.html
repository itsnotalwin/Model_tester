About the photographer

I photograph slowly. Most days I walk the same few kilometres of coastline and street, waiting for the hour when colour and weather finally agree. The work here comes from those walks — basalt dawns, tram windows, and the blue hour that Reykjavík does better than anywhere.

Trained as a printmaker, I still think in layers and paper; the camera came years later, the patience was already there. When I am not in the field, I am in my studio darkroom, or teaching small workshops on photographing the dark.
