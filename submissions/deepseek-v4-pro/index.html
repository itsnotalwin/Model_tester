About

The light decides.

Mara Lindqvist is a Reykjavík-based photographer working between landscape and the human figure — basalt coasts at dawn, strangers under neon, glaciers the colour of deep water. She shoots film and mirrorless, but always for the same thing: the instant the light makes up its mind.
