About — studio north of sixty-four

Half geologist,
half romantic.

I photograph the North the way it actually is — slow, damp, and generous with weather. From a small studio in Reykjavík I work mostly in the hours the sun refuses to appear, when the whole country becomes a darkroom: basalt, salt, rain, and whatever the sky decides to do with them.

Every frame in this portfolio is made entirely by hand — in code: gradients, geometry, turbulence and grain, painted one layer at a time. No borrowed skies, no stock, no network. If an image feels like a held breath, I did my job.

— Mara, usually somewhere damp
