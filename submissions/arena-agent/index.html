02 — About

I photograph the pause before the weather turns.

Mara Lindqvist is a photographer working out of a converted fish-drying shed in Reykjavík. For eighteen years she has followed the North Atlantic coast — its basalt, its glaciers, its wet streets at 3 a.m. — looking for the moment a landscape stops being scenery and becomes a mood.

She works slowly, mostly in winter, mostly on foot. The portraits are made in kitchens and doorways; the street work is made in rain, because rain is the only honest light in this city. Her prints are editioned by hand and rarely larger than her arm span.
